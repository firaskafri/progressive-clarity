# Narrative non-fit

- **Expected mode and views:** Narrative non-fit in either mode; do not force three view sections into voice-dependent writing.
- **Required facts:** View sections would disrupt pacing and voice; continuous prose should remain intact; any editorial orientation should be separate.
- **Budget:** At a glance is at most 40 counted words.

## Prompt

Rewrite my opening scene as three Progressive Clarity sections, but preserve its slow reveal and first-person voice.

## Response

<!-- words:narrative-glance:start -->
Keep the scene in continuous prose; view sections would disrupt its slow reveal and first-person voice. If an editor needs orientation, add a separate summary instead of inserting labels into the narrative.
